## Today

I lead Shopping Partner Engineering at Google: a global organization of 33 people, managers and engineers, across Europe, the US and Asia. We look after the technical success of Google's e-commerce API ecosystem, used by millions of merchants, from global retailers to e-commerce platforms.

In industry terms, that is partner and solutions engineering, close to what is now called forward-deployed engineering (FDE): I was an FDE before it was cool, and now I lead them. I advise partners up to C-level and spend much of my time coaching people and growing leaders.

I also stay hands-on. I build AI prototypes and AI-powered e-commerce demos with [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/), [WebMCP](https://webmachinelearning.github.io/webmcp/) and A2A, work on agentic commerce protocols such as [UCP](https://ucp.dev/), the Universal Commerce Protocol, and have trained 300+ engineers on AI tools. AI transformation, in e-commerce and well beyond it, is a topic I care a lot about.

## Path

I'm a mechanical engineer by training ([ICAM](https://www.icam.fr/), France) and went on to [London Business School](https://www.london.edu/) for a master's in management. I learned to code on the side, out of passion, and it became my job. Before Google, I was an early employee at [Rangespan](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-27261504), a London start-up later acquired by Google.

I joined Google in 2011 in Dublin, moved to London, and have been in Zurich since 2018. Home has also been France, Brazil, Canada and the US. I work in French and English, also speak German (B1), and hold a Swiss C permit. Most importantly, I'm a dad.
